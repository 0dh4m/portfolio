High-Protein Spiced Golden Chicken & Basmati Bowl

Prep: 10 mins · Cook: 15 mins · Level: Easy

680 kcal
Protein 52g 31% cal
Carbs 74g 44% cal
Fats 19g 25% cal

Precision Weights (Gram Accurate):

  • 200g Skinless Chicken Breast (Diced)
  • 80g Basmati Rice (Dry weight cooked in salted water)
  • 100g Baby Spinach (Sautéed with 5ml olive oil)
  • 120g Steamed Broccoli with lemon zest
  • 1 Whole Boiled Egg (For healthy HDL lipids)

Preparation Steps:

1. Season diced chicken breast with sweet paprika, turmeric, sea salt, and black pepper.
2. Sear chicken in a non-stick pan over medium heat for 6-8 minutes until golden.
3. Steam broccoli for 4 minutes to preserve micronutrients and crispness.
4. Assemble warm bowl over fluffy basmati rice, top with sliced egg and fresh spinach leaves.

PFE Project Dossier: Happy Gym PRO

MIAGE Rabat · Software Engineering Thesis (2025 – 2026)

Project Mission & Problem Formulation

Athletes face severe nutritional friction: manual calorie counting, inaccurate dietary estimates, and diet monotony. Happy Gym PRO solves this by unifying a web application (PHP 8, Tailwind CSS, JS ES6+) and a native Windows desktop app (C#, .NET, WPF) synchronized over a central relational MySQL database.
